How to read this. Pass rate counts sacks and scrambles as passes. EPA = expected points added; above 0 is good for the offense. Early downs, 3rd down, red zone and “all neutral” use only plays when the offense’s win probability was 5–95%. The 2-minute and trailing-by-8+ splits use every play. Numbers marked * are under 30 plays: treat them as a hint, not a tendency.
