How to read this. Pass rate counts sacks and scrambles as passes. Pass rate over expected compares their pass rate with what an average team calls in the same down, distance, field position, score and time (nflverse xpass model). EPA = expected points added; above 0 is good for the offense. Early downs, 3rd down, red zone and “all neutral” use only plays when the offense’s win probability was 5–95%. The 2-minute and trailing-by-8+ splits use every play. Numbers marked * are under 30 plays: treat them as a hint, not a tendency. Shift = last 4 games differ from the season by 5+ points (0.10 EPA) on 30+ plays.
