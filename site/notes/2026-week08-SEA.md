# Seattle, Week 8 (Bears at Seahawks, Mon Nov 2)
Draft: written from the data by Claude for Chris to edit. Numbers are through Week 7.
- Load up for the early-down run. They run more than the situation calls for (5 pts under expected, 26th) and line up under center more than almost anyone (45% shotgun, 28th), but the runs don't work: −0.26 EPA per run, 31st, on 145 plays. Make them beat us through the air on 1st and 2nd down.
- Their passing has cooled. Early-down dropbacks fell from +0.10 EPA on the season to −0.06 over the last 4 games (93 plays), and they've leaned even further toward the run (8 pts under expected in that stretch, 29th). Stop the run early and they're left with a passing game that's been below average for a month.
- Make them drive the length in the 2-minute drill. Their end-of-half offense is 2nd-worst in the league (−0.36 EPA per play, 31st, 58 plays) and still only 72% shotgun against 84% for the league. Keep everything in front and don't hand them free yards.
