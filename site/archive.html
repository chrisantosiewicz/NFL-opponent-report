How to read this. Each card is the scouting notes for one offense, condensed to one line per call, next to what happened in that game. Tendency calls (what they do) are a Hit when the game showed it. Warnings (a threat to take away, or a soft spot for us to attack) are graded on production, not volume: on defense "Held" means we took it away, since fewer targets can mean our coverage worked. "Mixed" means yards and EPA disagreed. "Didn't come up" means too few plays in the game to say. Calls use only games before that week; the grade uses that game alone.
