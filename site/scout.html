How to read this. Every dot is one NFL team, ranked on the same plays. The big dot is the team you picked, the dark line is the league average. Pass rate counts sacks and scrambles as passes. Pass rate over expected compares their pass rate with what an average team calls in the same down, distance, field position, score and time (nflverse xpass model). EPA is expected points added per play; above 0 helps the offense. Early downs, 3rd down, red zone and all neutral use only plays with the offense's win probability between 5% and 95%; 2-minute and trailing by 8+ use every play. Numbers on fewer than 30 plays are greyed and marked * and never make "What stands out". Only games before this week are used.
